## How to credit

Cite the benchmark dataset by its DOI once the Zenodo deposit exists (the
DOI will be recorded in `CITATION.cff` and in the README at release). Until
then, cite this repository by URL and commit hash.

## What is not ours to license

- Raw API responses from OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed, Dimensions and ROR are
  not redistributed in this repository. Each is covered by its provider's
  own terms; the ROR data dump is CC0 and OpenAlex is CC0, while Dimensions
  data is subject to its API terms. Where a provider's terms allow, raw
  snapshots are deposited alongside the release and that is stated there.
- Affiliation strings quoted in the gold standard are factual metadata
  from published works. Personal email addresses that publishers append
  to them have their local part removed before publication (see
  METHODS.md); the raw snapshot on disk is unmodified.
